Small Gummikins are the ones people keep on a desk, tuck into a coat pocket, or slip into a care package. They are small enough to go anywhere and big enough to feel like *someone*.

Each one is crocheted by hand in a continuous spiral, stuffed until it is squishy but holds its shape, and finished with two shiny eyes and a stitched cream smile.
