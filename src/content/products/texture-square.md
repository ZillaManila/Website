The Texture Square is a small blanket designed around how it feels. Rows of velvety chenille alternate with rows of raised stitches, so there is always something different under your fingertips.

It is easy to keep in a bag, on a desk, or in the car. Some people rub the edge, some trace the rows, some just keep it in their lap.
