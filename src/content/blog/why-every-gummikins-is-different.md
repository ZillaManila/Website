Every Gummikins starts the same way: a loop of yarn, a hook, and a spiral of stitches that grows round by round. But by the time it is finished, it has become its own little creature.

## Hands, not machines

When you crochet by hand, every stitch is a tiny decision. How tight to pull the yarn. When to add a stitch to make the body wider. When to stop stuffing. Those decisions change from day to day, and they show up in the finished piece.

Some Gummikins come out a little taller. Some sit a little lower. Some smiles curve up just a touch more on one side.

## The smile is always last

The smile is embroidered by hand at the very end, and it is the moment each Gummikins really becomes itself. We don't measure it with a ruler. We stitch it until it looks right, and *right* is a little different every time.

## What that means for you

When you order a Gummikins, it will look very close to the photos, but it won't be identical. It will be yours. And if you ever line up a few side by side, you'll see they are a family, not a set.
