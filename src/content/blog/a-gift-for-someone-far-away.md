Gummikins began as a way to send a hug through the mail, and that is still one of our favorite reasons people order one.

## Pick the size for how it will be held

- **Keychain** if they're always on the go. It rides along on keys, backpacks, and work badges.
- **Small** for a desk, a nightstand, or a care package.
- **Medium** for a couch companion or a car-ride buddy.
- **Large** if what they need most is something to hold on to.

## Pick a color that feels like them

There's no wrong answer. Some people choose a favorite color. Some choose a color that reminds them of home. Some get two in matching colors, one to send and one to keep.

## Add a note

At checkout, you can include a short gift note. We write it by hand and leave prices out of the box. Keep it simple: *Thinking of you. This one is here when I can't be.*
